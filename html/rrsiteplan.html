Overview

Purpose

Welcome to Reunited Roommates. The purpose of this website is that you are a detective looking for what happened with a group of friends at their 10 year college reunion. I'll give you a hint, someone's dead! Your job is to figure out why they died, who did it, and what evidence will prove that they're the culprit! Other than that these web pages will be for you to explore. All the clues are hidden within these pages so click around, explore, have fun, and find out who dun it!

Audience

If you like puzzles and hunting for clues, then this experience is the one for you! This is both a passion project and my final project for this Web Fundamentals class, but don't worry, I'll be making more exciting things in the future so look out world, cause here I come!
